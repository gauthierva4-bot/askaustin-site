Ask Austin Service, Cancellation and Refund Policy

Version 2026-11-05. Updated November 5, 2026. It applies only to purchases made after you accept it. Purchases made earlier keep the terms shown when you bought them.

1. General refund policy

All sales are final once your purchase is completed, except where applicable law or the payment provider's rules require otherwise. Change-of-mind refunds are not offered. If your purchased service is not delivered as promised, contact support for resolution, including a refund where appropriate. Apple processes refund requests for purchases billed through the App Store.

The specific remedies below (a late answer, or a request Austin declines) are part of what you buy. They are remedies for a service not delivered as promised, not change-of-mind refunds.

2. What happens if Austin is late (Message and Video)

  • Austin has 24 elapsed hours after your payment is verified to answer, and 24 hours after a Message follow-up to answer it.
  • If that time passes with no answer, a button in the chat lets you request a refund. You do not need to contact support first.
  • Message and video purchases are billed by Apple. We cannot send money back to your App Store account ourselves: the refund is complete only when Apple processes it. To ask Apple directly, use reportaproblem.apple.com. The chat shows "Refund requested. Waiting on Apple. This is not complete yet." until Apple confirms.
  • If Austin answers before you request a refund, the deadline remedy no longer applies to that answer.

3. If Austin declines a request

If Austin declines a paid message or video, a refund starts. Apple completes it (Section 2).

4. Things that are not refunds

  • Deleting your account does not refund a purchase. App Store refunds are handled by Apple.
  • A Video does not include a follow-up, and a Message includes only one. A new question needs a new purchase.
  • The service is baseball education. Disagreeing with Austin's advice, or not getting a result you hoped for, is not by itself non-delivery (see the Terms of Service, "No guaranteed results").

5. Payment provider and your other rights

  • Apple (messages and videos): Apple decides App Store refund requests under Apple's rules. You can always ask Apple directly at reportaproblem.apple.com, whether or not you also contact us.
  • Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under applicable law or under the payment provider's rules.

6. Contact

Email Austin James Gauthier at support@getaskaustin.com (or use Help in the app) with the email you signed in with and the chat involved.
