Ask Austin: sign-in code for App Review

For Apple's App Review team only. It works for the review account only.

  1. In the Ask Austin app, go to Account and tap Sign in, enter the review email from the App Review notes, and tap Email me a code.
  2. On this page, enter the review passcode from the App Review notes and tap Get my sign-in code.
  3. Type the code into the app and tap Sign in.
