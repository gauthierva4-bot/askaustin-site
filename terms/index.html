3. The services

Prices are shown in the app before you pay. Each purchase covers one service for one account.

Service What you get Follow-up Timing
Message (App Store purchase) You send one written question. Austin writes a personal answer. One follow-up question, which you can send during the 7 days after Austin's first answer. Austin answers it once more. Austin has 24 hours after your payment is verified to answer, and 24 hours after your follow-up to answer it. The clock is elapsed hours, not calendar or business days.

Declining a request. Austin may decline a question he cannot answer appropriately (for example, a question that needs a medical professional or that breaks Section 9). If he declines a paid request, a refund starts under the Service, Cancellation and Refund Policy.

Your information. Advice is based only on the information, descriptions and video you provide. Austin cannot see what you leave out, and feedback may be incomplete or wrong if the information is incomplete or inaccurate.

Feedback through the app cannot replace an in-person assessment by a qualified coach, athletic trainer, physician or other professional who can examine the athlete.

You (or, for a child, the parent or guardian) decide whether and how to apply any feedback, and are responsible for appropriate supervision, warm-up, workload, rest, equipment and a safe place to train. Children should apply training feedback only under adult supervision.
